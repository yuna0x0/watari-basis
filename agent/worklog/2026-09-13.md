## Allow Collision off dropped the listed colliders

A skirt PhysBone with two leg capsule colliders passed straight through them after conversion.
The rig carried both capsules, converted correctly (height 0.94 end to end became 0.54 centre
to centre), with `collisionToggle` off.

- Cause: the mapper turned collision off whenever `allowCollision` was false. In VRChat that
  field only governs colliders not listed on the component, which the SDK tooltip states are
  other players' hands (`VRC.Dynamics.dll`, `VRCPhysBoneBase.allowCollision`). The listed
  colliders apply regardless.
- With `collisionToggle` off, jiggle sets every point's `collisionRadius` to 0 and
  `DoDepenetration` returns on `worldRadius == 0`, so the colliders on the rig do nothing.
- Fix: the toggle follows the radius alone, as the Dynamic Bone and VRM mappers already did.
  `physbone.allowCollision.off` is now Approximated: `BasisAvatarDriver.AddJiggleRigColliders`
  registers every avatar's feet, arms, hands and fingers as global jiggle colliders, and
  `JiggleTreeJobData` has no per-rig opt-out, so the exclusion cannot be carried over.
- A rig converted before this fix keeps `collisionToggle` off; convert again or tick Collision
  on the JiggleRig.
